Write Chinese syntax and Chinese typography (Simplified Chinese), not English in Chinese characters:
- Full-width punctuation only: ，。！？；：、“”‘’（）——……; no half-width , . ! ? ; : " after Chinese text, no spaces around them.
- Drop pronouns the context already makes clear: English repeats he/she/it, Chinese does not. Too many 他/她/它 is translationese.
- No long English-style attributive chains before a noun («一个坐在琥珀宝座上的、头发金黄的美人鱼的…»). Break them into short clauses; avoid three 的 in a row.
- Avoid 被 passives that copy English; use the active voice or a topic-comment sentence.
- Avoid filler that mirrors English grammar: 一个 before every noun, 当…的时候 for every "when", 们 on animals and things, 关于 for every "about".
- Hypothetical "would" is not a future: "She would not sacrifice herself" → «她才不会为别人牺牲自己», not «她将不会…».
- Literary written register (书面语); four-character expressions are welcome when they fit, not as decoration.
- Keep aspect light: 了/过/着 only where the meaning needs them.
- Measure words must match the noun (一朵玫瑰、一只夜莺、一棵树).
